# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 13, 2026

Plain Journal ("the app") is a private journal and daily tracking application
developed by Logan. This policy explains what information the app collects and
how it is used.

## Summary

Plain Journal is designed to be **private by default**. Your journal entries,
mood logs, food records, tags, and period data stay **on your device** and are
never transmitted to us or to any third party.

## Information we do not collect

- No account or sign-up is required.
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any contact data.
- We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
- We do not show ads.
- We do not sell, rent, or share any user data.

## Information stored on your device

Everything you type or select inside the app is stored locally on your device
using the device's standard app storage:

- Journal notes and written content
- Mood selections and tags
- Food / meal records
- Period (menstrual cycle) markers
- Your selected city for weather
- Derived statistics (e.g. streaks, mood charts)

Because data is stored on-device, anyone with access to your unlocked device can
view it. **Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data.** Before
uninstalling, export anything you wish to keep.

## Weather information

To show weather forecasts, the app sends your **selected city name and its
coordinates** to the Open-Meteo weather API (https://open-meteo.com). This is a
free, no-key API. Open-Meteo's privacy policy applies to that request. We do not
store or transmit your precise location; no GPS/location permission is used.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Cloud sync (future)

A future update may add optional cloud backup/sync. If and when that is
introduced, this policy will be updated to describe exactly what data would be
transferred and how it would be protected, and the feature will remain opt-in.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be reflected in the
version linked from the app's Google Play listing.
